# Refract Roadmap

Static, read-only public roadmap for Refract. Suggestions and vote counts come
from Discord; voting and discussion stay on Discord.

The site has no build step and is served directly by GitHub Pages. For a local
preview, serve this directory over HTTP and open `index.html`. `roadmap.json` is
generated by the private Refract bot repository and is refreshed by the VM
every twelve hours.

The site is published at `https://jeremyndeby.github.io/refract-roadmap/`. The
public tree contains only the static site, its logo, and the generated roadmap
data.

Shipped entries with a verified delivery month are shown newest first. Older
entries without a recoverable delivery date are kept visible in the final
`Earlier` section.

Open suggestions also expose their total votes and their change over the last
seven days. The history displays as unavailable until the private generator has
accumulated a full week of snapshots. `🔥 Hottest` sorts by this recent change;
the initial hot badge threshold is 10 gained votes and will be calibrated once
real weekly data is available.
